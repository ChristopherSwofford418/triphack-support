Destination guide did not finish

Confirm that your device has an internet connection, then tap Try again. Failed generations do not use one of your two free guides.

Restore TripHack Unlimited

Open Settings → Restore Purchases or use the Restore Purchases button on the paywall. Use the same Apple ID that originally purchased the subscription.

Manage or cancel the subscription

Open Settings → Manage Subscription, or manage subscriptions from your Apple ID settings. Apple controls billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds.

Export or delete local data

Open Settings to export all local app data as JSON or permanently delete local travel records. Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Travel-information limits

TripHack provides planning inspiration, not live inventory, booking, medical, legal, visa, weather, or safety advice. Verify time-sensitive details with providers and official authorities before travel.

Still need help?

Email info@clearpasstechnologies.com. Include your device type, iOS version, and a short description. Do not send payment-card details, passport data, or sensitive personal documents.

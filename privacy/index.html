Privacy policy

Your plans belong to you.

Effective September 27, 2026. TripHack is provided by ClearPass Technologies LLC.

1. Our approach

TripHack is guest-first and local-first. We do not sell personal information, run advertising profiles, or steer plans through affiliate commissions.

2. Information kept on your device

Your saved trip library, generated guides, selected activities, private notes, checklists, and preferences are stored locally in the app. Sensitive local records are protected using device-managed secure storage and app encryption.

TripHack does not ask for your email address, contacts, reservation confirmations, live or background location, passport data, visa status, citizenship, or device-calendar contents.

3. AI destination guides

When you request a guide, TripHack sends the destination name, selected trip length, travel style, and group type to TripHack’s server and AI-processing provider so the requested guide can be generated. Standard network and operational security data may be processed when that request is transmitted.

  • Private notes, photos, checklists, calendar data, and precise location are not sent for guide generation.
  • The generated result is saved locally so you can reopen it offline.
  • Destination requests are not sold or used to build advertising profiles.
  • AI suggestions can be incomplete or outdated and should be verified with official sources.

4. Diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostics are optional and disabled by default. If enabled, TripHack uses a random installation identifier and an allowlisted event schema. Diagnostics do not include destination names, notes, photos, coordinates, or identity. You can disable diagnostics at any time in Settings.

5. Apple subscriptions

Apple processes subscription purchases and payment information. TripHack does not receive or store payment card details. The app reads the current StoreKit entitlement for TripHack Unlimited to determine access. For non-subscribers, a device-level counter records how many of the two successful free guides have been used; it does not contain destination names or account identity.

6. Retention, export, and deletion

Saved trips and guides remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Settings provides controls to export app data and delete local data. Deleting local travel records does not cancel an Apple subscription or reset the service allowance. Apple subscriptions must be managed separately in your App Store account.

7. Children

TripHack is not directed to children under 13, does not offer social features, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as TripHack changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

9. Contact

For support, privacy questions, or requests, email info@clearpasstechnologies.com.
